### Caveats stated in the results

- Scores are on a synthetic task whose demands are built from an anchor section's capacity, and the ratio features are close to first-order capacity checks, so they are an upper bound, not expected real-world accuracy.
- The classifier alone is not safe (non-zero false-pass rate above); every recommendation is re-checked by System A before it is returned.
- Out-of-range means longer lengths only (30.5-45 ft). Figure: `presentation/system_b_baseline.png`.
